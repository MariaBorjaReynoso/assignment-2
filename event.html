Fall Concert

Music · Friday, Sep 25, 2026, 7:00 PM · Cone Ballroom

Join us for a night of live music featuring student bands and a special guest. It is the biggest music event of the semester.

Event Details

Doors open at 6:30 PM. The show runs until 10:00 PM. The event is free for students with a valid ID.

What to Expect

Come ready to dance and meet other students. Food trucks will be outside, and there will be a photo booth near the entrance.

Schedule

  • 6:30 PM — Doors open
  • 7:00 PM — Opening act
  • 8:00 PM — Student bands
  • 9:00 PM — Special guest
  • 10:00 PM — Event ends

Accessibility

The Cone Ballroom is wheelchair accessible. If you need ASL interpretation, please email us at least 72 hours in advance.

Event Info

Date: Friday, Sep 25, 2026

Time: 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Location: Cone Ballroom

Organizer: Student Engagement

Admission: Free with student ID
